## Canonical names and conflicts

The strongest current official list is UNILAG's March 2026 hall-executive notice. It names **El-Kanemi, Eni-Njoku, Fagunwa, Femi Gbajabiamila, King Jaja, Professor Saburi Biobaku, Madam Kofo Ademola, Madam Tinubu, Makama Bida, Mariere, Moremi, Sodeinde, and Queen Amina**. Confidence: high. Source: [UNILAG Students' Union inauguration](https://unilag.edu.ng/unilag-revives-students-union-after-10-year-hiatus-inaugurates-renaissance-administration/), 24 March 2026.

UNILAG's May 2025 accommodation notice separately lists **Mariere, Eni-Njoku, Saburi Biobaku, Shodeinde, Makama Bida, Queen Amina, and Honours**. Its June 2025 movement notice lists **Moremi, Fagunwa, Kofo Ademola, Madam Tinubu, and Jaja**. Confidence: high for what those notices said at those dates. Sources: [May accommodation notice](https://unilag.edu.ng/important-notice-student-accommodation-update/), 13 May 2025; [June movement notice](https://unilag.edu.ng/important-notice-update-on-movement-into-halls-of-residence/), 2 June 2025.

Alias ledger: `Mariere` is preferred; `Marierie` and `Mareire` occur as typos. `Sodeinde` is the 2026 form; `Sodehinde` and `Shodeinde` occur in older material. `King Jaja` and `Jaja` both occur. `Professor Saburi Biobaku` is often shortened to `Biobaku`. `Madam Kofo Ademola` and `Kofoworola Ademola` both occur. `Honours` and `Honors` vary. These are aliases to retain for search/import, not separate halls.

**Queen Amina, Eni-Njoku, Sodeinde, Makama Bida, Fagunwa, El-Kanemi, and Honours are separate names.** No source found supports combining them. “New Hall Complex” is also a separate complex reference; it must not be treated as a synonym for Queen Amina without evidence.

The 2026 list adds **Femi Gbajabiamila**, absent from the 2025 accommodation notices. Record it as current-list evidence with a date, while keeping its operational status and category pending confirmation. Confidence: high for the list, unknown for those details.

## Academic and facilities evidence

The official 1 August 2025 reorganisation announcement says UNILAG expanded from 12 to 19 faculties and lists the new structure. Confidence: high for the announcement, with page-level consistency still pending. Source: [When the Future Calls](https://unilag.edu.ng/when-the-future-calls-unilag-answers-with-new-faculties-and-a-new-vision/), 1 August 2025.

The Faculty of Engineering homepage lists Biomedical, Chemical, Civil & Environmental, Electrical & Electronics, Mechanical, Metallurgical & Materials, Surveying & Geoinformatics, and Systems Engineering. The older admissions catalogue also lists Chemical & Petroleum and Computer Engineering. The official 5 August 2025 comment on the reorganisation page says Computer Engineering is housed within Electrical & Electronics Engineering; model Computer Engineering as a **programme**, not an independent department, until a newer official department page says otherwise. Confidence: high for this modelling decision. Sources: [Faculty of Engineering](https://engineering.unilag.edu.ng/); [Admissions programmes](https://admissions.unilag.edu.ng/programmes.html); [reorganisation notice](https://unilag.edu.ng/when-the-future-calls-unilag-answers-with-new-faculties-and-a-new-vision/).

The Learning Resources document says Akoka has a two-floor main library, faculty/department libraries, engineering workshops, engineering design centres, innovation hubs, makerspaces, and digital resources including IEEE Xplore. Confidence: high for the document's stated resources. Source: [Learning Resources Effectiveness PDF](https://unilag.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Learning-Resources-Effectiveness.pdf), 2025.

The Student Life Cycle Guidelines identify the medical service, Akoka security office at the Faculty of Education, MAD House, UNILAG Museum, NiTHuB, botanical garden, Confucius Institute, Sports Complex, Lagoon Front and Moremi/Mariere information screen. They also state that undergraduate hall allocation uses online balloting and remains inadequate. Confidence: high for the guideline claims. Source: [Student Life Cycle Guidelines PDF](https://unilag.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Student-Life-Cycle-GUIDELINES-Hyperlinked.pdf), 2025.

UNILAG's official cafeteria notice identifies the **2001 Cafeteria** and explicitly places it at the **New Hall Complex** on Main Campus, Akoka. This is evidence for the complex and cafeteria only; it does not identify New Hall with Queen Amina. Confidence: high. Source: [NBC rebrands UNILAG Main Cafeteria](https://unilag.edu.ng/nbc-rebrands-unilag-main-cafeteria/), 6 June 2024.

The 2025 accommodation notices use `Shodeinde`, while the 2026 official hall list uses `Sodeinde`; preserve both as aliases and prefer the 2026 form. `Jaja` versus `King Jaja`, `Biobaku` versus `Professor Saburi Biobaku`, and `Kofo Ademola` versus `Kofoworola Ademola` are shortening/formality differences supported by official pages. ## Layout decisions and remaining uncertainty

Coordinates in `layout.js` use x east and z south over a 600 by 480 unit compressed campus, plus the lagoon promenade. They are deliberately authored placements. Confidence flags refer to source relationships, not metre accuracy. Ten zones preserve major compass relationships. Roads connect navigable public areas; their widths, bends and shuttle route are beta choices, not a verified current shuttle service. Water starts east of x=340 and cannot be walked on.

Queen Amina is separate from New Hall and lies south of Education. Clinical/Dental/Basic Medical buildings were excluded from Akoka because the evidence does not place those faculties here. No Dan Fodio residence hall was invented. Student Union and Bookshop exist in campus inventories, but their internal placement remains low confidence. The arrangement of halls within New Hall, exact Second Gate form, lecture theatre floorplans, Access Bank frontage, amphitheatre seating, and DLI compound boundaries need stronger visual evidence. Femi Gbajabiamila Hall is in the current official list but is not placed without location evidence. Henry Carr, bank branches beyond Access, a separately modelled pool/cricket oval, and actual terrain/shoreline contours remain geometry gaps.

## Integration requests

The campus lane does not edit existing files. The parity owner must register `UNILAG_VENUE` in the venue catalogue, `buildUnilag` for scene kind `unilag`, and campus systems in the registry. The preview is isolated from the game server; no production delivery is claimed.

The current `src/scene/crowd.js` clamps positions to 20 units, and the server position protocol is venue-sized. Campus positions need a per-venue range and walk validation using campus bounds, without weakening other venue validation. Host walking should use the scene's coarse-plus-fine `walk.grid.path` adapter. Camera occlusion receives `walk.solids` including heights. Campus LOD changes only from `setPosition`/`walk.move`, never a timer.

The owner must wire the Campus phone app to the student view, the existing Bank phone app at `access-bank`, existing shared table games at Student Union, transactional shared elections/leaderboards, city travel arrival at Main Gate, and analytics events at the UI host. The campus rule modules emit domain events and do not dispatch DOM events. Integration event example: `window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('jaw:track', {detail:{name, props}}))`.
