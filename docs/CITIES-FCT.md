# Abuja and the Federal Capital Territory

Abuja opens the six FCT area councils: Abuja Municipal (AMAC), Bwari, Gwagwalada, Kuje, Kwali and Abaji. The administrative source calls AMAC Municipal Area Council. Local units are displayed as area councils throughout the city. The play footprint covers the FCT and shares its exact outline with the state overview.

A new character arrives at the fictional beta Kubwa Community Garden. Kubwa’s mapped town reference and the default rental are inside Bwari. Every area council offers a free starter estate. Fourteen rentals use verified district or town references, including Central Business District, Garki, Wuse, Maitama, Asokoro, Gwarinpa, Jabi, Utako, Lugbe and Kubwa. These points represent neighbourhoods; they do not identify real rental properties.

The catalogue supplies 28 public venues, 56 fictional regulars and all 15 career workplaces. National worship landmarks use respectful visitor scenes. The clinic includes the shared paid check-up and free treatment queue. The crafts activity introduces Gbagyi pottery; the culture card also recognises the FCT’s other original peoples and residents from across Nigeria. Suya, kilishi, multilingual greetings, parks and harmattan give the city its local character.

## Public landmarks and exterior context

Playable public scenes include Millennium Park, Jabi Lake Park, the National Mosque, National Christian Centre, Eagle Square, the Arts and Crafts Village, Wuse and Garki markets, MKO Abiola National Stadium and its Velodrome, the National Children’s Park and Zoo, three university references, the airport, Idu station and the city gate.

Presidential Villa, National Assembly, Supreme Court and the Three Arms Zone are exterior map references only. Aso Rock is an exterior context marker, with no climbing or protected-site entry activity. Jabi Lake and Usuma Dam retain water markers; Usuma public access is not asserted. Zuma Rock is labelled Niger State and remains a non-enterable context marker. The pinned simplified gbOpen boundary places that rock reference inside its FCT outline, a disclosed source disagreement rather than a change to the geographic label.

University of Abuja uses the mapped Giri main-campus reference. That point belongs to AMAC in the pinned ADM2 source. The university’s association with Gwagwalada does not move this point into another council; no separately verified Gwagwalada mini-campus gate is claimed. Nile University uses a mapped campus car-park reference. All OSM polygon centres are qualified as feature references, not surveyed entrances. Jabi Lake Park uses the published park coordinate in an Ahmadu Bello University field study; the coordinate dates to the study’s 2008 sampling and is not a surveyed current gate. FCTA tourism independently identifies the park.

## Zuma Rock jurisdiction and boundary data

The [Federal Ministry of Information tourism guide](https://fmino.gov.ng/culture/tourism/) places Zuma Rock in Niger State. The [Niger State government](https://nigerstate.gov.ng/about/) and [state investment agency](https://nsipa.nigerstate.gov.ng/tourism/) also identify it as a Niger attraction. Its mapped OSM rock centroid is retained at 7.2341492 E, 9.1305765 N.

The pinned geoBoundaries outline includes that point in FCT/Gwagwalada. OSM administrative relations 3717259 and 3717265 also include it in FCT, but assign the lower-level area to Municipal Area Council; those relations cite a 2013 source. These generalized boundary layers do not resolve the jurisdiction discrepancy. The map therefore labels Zuma as a non-enterable Niger State context landmark, explicitly notes the boundary-data mismatch, and does not infer an Abuja council label for it. Neither the rock point nor the administrative polygons are moved to hide the disagreement.
