It does not change the character’s city, local government, home, estate or map location. The exact Tiga-rock geographic point remains unverified.

Air distances are rounded great-circle reference distances between sourced airport points, not aircraft routing or published schedules.

Dated reports describe restrictions on major roads and overnight operation; no unsourced current curfew rule is asserted. Commercial okada is not offered.

These labels use primary seasonal evidence, while the monthly probabilities are original game choices. Existing seeded weather timing remains shared, with no new dust or heat penalties.
