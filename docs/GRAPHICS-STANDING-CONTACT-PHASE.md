# Standing contact and animation pose reset

The previous venue policy rejected contact whenever either foot touched a different support height. Standing across supported 3cm/6cm paving therefore left both feet unfitted. The policy now preflights every sole sample, rejects unsupported/nonfinite ground, and independently assigns each side its highest supported target. Easing, seated poses, swing gating, root position and per-pass anatomical correction limits remain intact. A sole straddling a step may bridge the lower surface; this does not implement full stair navigation.

Foot fitting also exposed a separate animation defect: Three.js can skip a property write when the sampled clip/time is unchanged, leaving external IK edits on the skeleton. Each body now saves its raw mixer pose before transition blending and restores it before the next sample. On clip changes restoration happens before stopping the old action, allowing stop() to reset tracks omitted from the next clip. Saved vectors are allocated once per body and reused; no timer or animation loop is added.
