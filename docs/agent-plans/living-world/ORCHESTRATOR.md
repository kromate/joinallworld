## Repeatable delivery loop

1. Play the actual current app and relevant missions; run appropriate simulations. Capture unmet planned requirements and reproducible player problems, including zero-money recovery, progression and reconnect behavior. Rank them by player impact within the agreed gameplay, real-business and Goalmatic scope.
2. Choose one finite batch with a complete player outcome, exclusive write scopes, pass/fail observations and bounded effort. Implement it and meaningful behavioral tests.
3. Test, exercise the actual UI, diagnose, repair and retest until acceptance passes or a concrete blocker is established. Preserve Node/Worker parity, compatible saves, authority, durable receipts/domain uniqueness and consent. Test solo/NPC and multiplayer, mobile/desktop, restart/reconnect, races, revocation/departure and stock/cash accounting. Distinguish physical-device evidence from viewport emulation.
4. Obtain independent review, repair accepted findings and recheck the integrated SHA. Stage the candidate and prepare the release packet required by PHASES.md. If a failure persists after one evidence-backed correction, investigate its cause or escalate the bounded issue; do not loop unchanged retries or weaken checks.
5. Deploy the accepted phase/batch through the existing production process within the user's standing phased-deployment assignment. Verify the actual live build, full journey and saved-progress continuity, then observe the defined failure thresholds and use only authorized recovery.
6. Update the evidence tracker, ranked backlog and checkpoint; play the result and select the next supported improvement or unmet phase requirement. Do not stop because the first slice passed. If no valuable supported work remains, record `awaiting direction`; do not invent tasks, claim a background runner exists, or spend indefinitely to keep a loop alive. Respect a user pause/stop.

Keep fictional qualifications and medicine/law/civic roles separate from real credentials and operator powers. Verify privacy, map/media licences and merchant consent. Keep real NGN, simulated points and Goalmatic credits separate. Do not overwrite player data or bypass provider limits.

Record the user's implementation assignment and standing production scope. Do not ask for generic approval again before every release. Existing deployment rules and action-time approvals still apply. New secret access, new costs/provider commitments, destructive/incompatible data changes, security/auth/domain changes and work outside scope require the applicable explicit authorization. A source/artifact change requires fresh relevant evidence; an expansion of authority or risk requires the relevant approval. No documentation can override a denied action.

Report each accepted batch with exact branch/SHA, observed outcome, tests/review, staging and production evidence, live observation results, blockers and next action. Keep incomplete requirements explicit. Never present plans, mocks, source code, builds or provider catalogues as deployed capability.
