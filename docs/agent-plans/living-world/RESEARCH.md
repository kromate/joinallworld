# Research and decision register

Checked 8 October 2026. This register separates source statements from proposed game/product decisions. Official guidance may change; recheck the relevant source before importing data, activating merchants or releasing payments. It is not a legal opinion or compliance certification.

| [Google Places API policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) | Storage/caching restrictions have specified exceptions; place IDs have a distinct exception. Map display and attribution rules apply. | Do not build a permanent custom 3D business database by scraping Places content. Use independently licensed data or owner submissions. A stored ID does not license copied photos, reviews or listing content. Review the actual applicable terms before any permitted integration. |
| [OpenStreetMap copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) | OSM data is under ODbL, with attribution and licence requirements. ## Decision record

The connected district, separate qualifications/ownership/permissions, NPC fallback, consent-bound barber service, and limited civic powers are product proposals. They are not asserted requirements from the sources above.

The curated merchant-link pilot reduces integration scope but still needs accurate claims, media rights, privacy notices and support handling. Native booking and payment are separate later releases with stronger evidence. A split-payment product must not be marketed as escrow without a separately reviewed contractual/provider basis.

Source documents describe current systems imperfectly. Use the [pinned baseline inventory](BASELINE.md) to locate code, then establish current behavior with source and runtime evidence. Historical prototypes, older city counts, capability catalogues and plans are not delivery proof.
