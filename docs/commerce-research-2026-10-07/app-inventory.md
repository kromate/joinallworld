## Store Studio: strongest commerce reuse

Store Studio is the only inspected App with a coherent commerce transaction boundary. Its architecture says Goalmatic Tables own `stores`, `products`, `orders`, `customers`, `discounts`, `movements`, and `imports`; Core commerce operations validate installation/account/store ownership and coordinate checkout effects atomically (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:15-21`, `docs/GOALMATIC_INTEGRATION.md:44-54`). The [manifest generator](/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/mini/apps/store-studio/scripts/manifest.mjs:5) confirms those seven logical Tables and the owner operation list. Physical Table IDs are installation-bound; browser code passes logical names such as `products`, never physical IDs (`docs/GOALMATIC_INTEGRATION.md:46-54`).

The reusable operation surface declared in source is:

- Catalog/checkout: `commerce.catalog-get`, `commerce.checkout-quote`, `commerce.checkout-create`.
- Orders: `commerce.order-get`, `commerce.order-update`, `commerce.test-payment`.
- Products/inventory: `commerce.product-save`, `commerce.product-archive`, `commerce.inventory-adjust`, `commerce.reservations-expire`.
- Store/theme: `commerce.store-save`, `commerce.theme-save`, `commerce.store-launch`.
- Customers/promotions/imports: `commerce.customer-save`, `commerce.discount-save`, `commerce.products-import`.
- Payments/refunds/digital delivery: `commerce.payment-bind`, `commerce.payment-start`, `commerce.payment-refresh`, `commerce.refund-create`, `commerce.refund-refresh`, `commerce.digital-download`, `commerce.media-resolve`.

The operation names are documented at `docs/GOALMATIC_INTEGRATION.md:56-74`; the [manifest list](/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/mini/apps/store-studio/scripts/manifest.mjs:15) declares them locally. The [runtime client](/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/mini/apps/store-studio/src/platform/client.js:88) dispatches through injected `GoalmaticApp.execute`, with bounded requests and idempotency/recovery. Integration review found matching definitions and implementations in [Core's imported commerce catalog](/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/go/gm/backend/functions/src/platform/commerce/catalog.ts:4) and runtime; see [platform-contracts.md](platform-contracts.md). This confirms local implementation, not deployment. Pages and components demonstrate merchant routes for products, inventory, orders, customers, discounts, analytics, storefront, settings, and imports; for example `pages/Products.vue`, `pages/Orders.vue`, `pages/Customers.vue`, and `pages/Discounts.vue`. Public routes are declared in the [manifest](/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/mini/apps/store-studio/scripts/manifest.mjs:92): `/s/:slug`, `/s/:slug/products/:productSlug`, `/s/:slug/cart`, `/s/:slug/checkout`, and `/s/:slug/orders/:reference`, with bounded guest actions including catalog, quote, checkout, order lookup, payment status, test payment, and digital download (`scripts/manifest.mjs:127-203`).

Payment support is platform-owned Paystack connection plumbing, not an App wallet. `MerchantPayments.vue` lists connected Paystack accounts and binds one through `/integrations` and `/payments/bind` (`components/MerchantPayments.vue:13-48`). `OrderPaymentLink.vue` requests payment start/refresh with an idempotency key and accepts only an HTTPS `checkout.paystack.com` URL (`components/merchant/OrderPaymentLink.vue:66-117`). The architecture explicitly separates an invoice from proof of payment and says Paystack connection, provider calls, refunds, webhooks, settlement, private download, and cross-installation denial still need integrated proof (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:51-61`; `docs/GOALMATIC_INTEGRATION.md:118-134`).

Reuse recommendation for Allworld: reuse the platform operations and transaction principles (server-authoritative totals, stock/reservation effects, idempotency, installation-bound resources, guest route grants). Existing merchants should keep their existing Store installation as the source of truth where the supported authorization path permits it. New merchants need appropriately provisioned resources. Do not copy private modules or share raw Tables across installation boundaries. An in-world listing can point to an authorized public storefront; a native purchase panel needs a supported scoped runtime bridge. Store Studio's orders and customers are not an Allworld player ledger, and payment settlement is distinct from in-game naira.

## Bookins: useful service and engagement primitives

Bookins owns its UI and pure scheduling logic while Goalmatic owns identity, Tables, grants, execution, integrations, credits, and lifecycle (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:3-5`). Its logical records are `profiles`, `schedules`, `services`, `bookings`, optional `contacts`, optional `staff`, and optional `campaigns` (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:7-17`). The [Bookins runtime](/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/mini/apps/bookins/booking.js:88) lists those Tables and uses idempotent `submit`, `update`, and `remove` calls (`booking.js:276-328`).

Its public route is `/book`, with four named guest actions in the documented [Bookins architecture](/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/mini/apps/bookins/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:33): profile/service link subjects, `page-get`, `openings-list`, `booking-create`, and optional `calendar-busy` (`booking.js:870-915`, `1010-1027`, `1188-1201`). Booking collision protection uses a schedule/time reservation key and explicit blocked time-off records (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:21-27`). The useful reuse for Allworld is appointment/slot logic, expiring scoped public grants, account-scoped records, and owner-opened communications. The source explicitly says messages and campaigns open WhatsApp/SMS/email links and never claim delivery; offers are text and Bookins does not take payments (`README.md:44-56`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:37-49`).

Bookins therefore does not provide a seller checkout, payout, payment link, invoice settlement, guest payment, or revenue ledger. Google Calendar is optional and source-declared through Goalmatic integration operations (`booking.js:960-1050`, `1073-1159`), but the README says real-account Calendar and daily-agenda behavior are unproven (`README.md:58-73`). Do not reuse Bookins bookings as Allworld orders without a new contract: its records are appointments, its prices are display estimates, and its guest confirmation is on-screen only.

## Reuse limits and acceptance gaps

App source is not an installable shared library. Every App has installation-owned resources, package/manifest bindings, permissions, route grants, and release identity. Copying UI or private modules would bypass those bindings; reuse should happen through stable Goalmatic operations or a new Core contract, with each App declaring its own resources. `apps/projects/*` are separate repositories and must not be imported as sibling implementation files (`/Users/anthonyakpan/Desktop/go/apps/AGENTS.md:3-15`).

The proposal must keep three balances separate: (1) Allworld game cash, the in-game naira wallet used by player/world mechanics; (2) fiat commerce earnings and settlement owed to sellers, requiring a real payment/ledger/payout contract; and (3) Goalmatic metered credits for billable App/agent/workflow actions. App installation pricing and entitlements are a separate Store/package question, not a fourth balance and never inferable from credits. Store Studio and Goalmatic Core provide locally implemented merchant payment operations; this does not prove a live connection, payout, refund, entitlement, or revenue-share contract. Bookins contributes no payment capability. Allworld still needs merchant discovery/binding, identity decisions, settlement reporting, fulfillment policy and live proof at the exact provider and installation boundaries.
