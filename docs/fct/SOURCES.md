# FCT catalogue sources

Retrieved and checked 5 October 2026. Source facts are paraphrased; no institutional photographs, maps or descriptive prose are reproduced.

## Administrative and map data

The [FCTA FAQ](https://www.fcta.gov.ng/faq/) confirms six area councils and their names. The map uses the pinned Nigeria geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM1/ADM2 source under CC BY 4.0; the generated FCT header records source URLs, checksums, projection and topology. Original source membership and derived dry land are checked separately for every public venue and rental. The display name Abuja Municipal (AMAC) maps to Municipal Area Council in that source.

Roads, water, rail and OSM landmark references are [OpenStreetMap contributor data](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), licensed ODbL 1.0. The Nigeria extract is dated 3 October 2026 and its SHA-256 is `6dfa568106792fb8a9bba0b12a91e1edfb789d38c452087b2c48bc58326880b5`. Individual landmark URLs, coordinates and accuracy appear in `src/game/cities/abuja/landmarks.ts`. Relations use the mapped outer feature reference; they do not claim a full compound centroid or entrance survey.

Housing and fictional service positions use these OSM locality references: [Kubwa](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3199516653), [Gwagwalada](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/501471969), [Kuje](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3615289126), [Kwali](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/12984989987), [Abaji](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/3615288382), [Central Business District](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/12193653), [Garki](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8329790550), [Wuse](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8329698302), [Maitama](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8329790549), [Asokoro](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8329790546), [Gwarinpa](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8337413720), [Jabi](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8337332089), [Utako](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/8335781694) and [Lugbe bus-stop locality](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/14024356419). No actual property listing or real service operator is implied.

## Public landmark identity and access

FCTA’s tourism catalogue documents [Jabi Lake and its park](https://www.fcta.gov.ng/ova_por/jabi-lake/), [Lower Usuma Dam](https://www.fcta.gov.ng/ova_por/lower-usuma-dam/) and [Abuja City Gate](https://www.fcta.gov.ng/ova_por/abuja-city-gate/). These are factual identity references, not reusable map datasets. Usuma remains a context marker because a public entrance has not been established.

Jabi Lake Park’s land reference is longitude `7.417552471160889`, latitude `9.073736071586609`, Table 3.1 of Ishiyaku Musa Mohammed’s [2014 Ahmadu Bello University thesis](https://kubanni-backend.abu.edu.ng/server/api/core/bitstreams/1f2bd815-1b2a-42ea-bc25-7a712a3bbc30/content). Sampling was performed in 2008. Only the numerical coordinate fact is used; no copyrighted prose, imagery or map is bundled. The point is verified on mapped land in AMAC but does not claim to be a surveyed current entrance.

[Smithsonian National Museum of African Art](https://africa.si.edu/collection/object/nmafa_2002-14-12) documents Gbagyi ceramic practice and the connection with the historical Abuja Pottery Training Centre, now in Suleja. The game learning session is fictional and does not relocate that historical centre to the Arts and Crafts Village. [FCTA resettlement information](https://www.fcta.gov.ng/ova_dep/resettlement-and-compensation/) distinguishes original inhabitants. Cultural activities and resident dialogue are original game writing.

## Closed Kaduna preview

The Kaduna atlas reference is [OSM city node 27565091](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/27565091), longitude `7.4359863`, latitude `10.5182899`. Rigasa terminal uses [OSM station-building way 488660318](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/488660318), mapped feature centroid `7.3551313`, `10.5487356`; both are ODbL 1.0 references. The [Nigerian Railway Corporation](https://nrc.gov.ng/akts/) documents the Abuja–Kaduna service. [NSIB](https://nsib.gov.ng/news/4f8a59de-1f5f-476a-81dd-18c0b76c8e22) identifies Rigasa in Kaduna and Idu in Abuja as the endpoints. The game fare, timer and distance are provisional and do not reproduce an operator’s timetable.
