Fictional clinics, kitchens, salons, savings halls, gardens, film workshop, transport-information hub, community house and polling room are beta services at mapped neighbourhood references, not real operators or surveyed properties. Their regulars, quotes, radio slot, billboard slots, tables, career placements, visit wishes and local dream wording are original beta content. Shared career pay, ladders, skill requirements, family loans, starter rewards, election rules and free starter-home mechanics remain unchanged. “Programme DJ” changes a role label only.

Air kilometre values are calculated reference distances. Road and rail kilometre quotes remain approximate beta planning values. Coming journeys cannot be started, and no line on the map is invented to turn a partial local railway into a complete national route. No dust or heat penalties are added.

The countryside outing is a fictional hosted simulation from the railway-station forecourt. Its numeric fields, label, effects and cooldown carry `beta: true`. Normal clock-based need decay continues during the timer. Completing or cancelling leaves city, local government, residence and location unchanged. It does not assert a real service, operator, Tiga Dam admission or a verified rock visit.
