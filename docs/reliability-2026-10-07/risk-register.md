# Reliability risk register

Evidence labels: **S** means source-observed behavior; **D** means a design risk inferred from that behavior; **O** means an operational fact requiring live verification. Priorities are programme sequencing, not claims of an active incident: **P0** before claiming financial/recovery safety, **P1** before substantial traffic growth, **P2** before the relevant feature or scale tier launches.

Paths are relative to the repository root. Line anchors describe the inspected revision and may drift. Each row names the work package that owns its solution. Acceptance details are in [implementation.md](./implementation.md).

## Coverage boundaries

This is an architectural/source review, not a penetration test or a proof that every branch is safe. No live attack, production load test, provider console audit, payment execution, mobile device test, or disaster restore occurred. W0 and the acceptance gates convert these unknowns into evidence. The article does not establish that D1 or Cloudflare is intrinsically unsuitable; Allworld uses SQLite-backed Durable Objects, and workload shape is the issue.
