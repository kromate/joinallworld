## Implementation sequence

1. **Money and recovery:** finish the complete Business quote contract, honest ambiguous-response feedback and actor-scoped cache/retry proof. Reproduce loading/credit/debt reports against authoritative receipts. Integrate with the separate reliability owner; no save or wealth reset.
2. **Messaging:** integrate the voice checkpoint against current source and prove private recording/send/play/retry/delete on Node/Worker and supported browsers. Add individual-message pinning with membership/moderator permissions and tombstone cleanup. Keep it separate from chat-list pins.
3. **Households and safe collaboration:** model member invitations, durable residence/edit grants, contributions, exit/revocation and original-home retention. Compare two or three UI prototypes before choosing the interaction, as requested by the design skill. Add owner-reviewed commissions whose timer starts with actual work, not merely hiring. Persist accepted edits while the owner is offline; test block/delete/unlink cleanup and two actors/restart.
4. **Asset transfers and commerce:** add voluntary recipient-approved furniture/home/business transfers only after ownership and wallet contracts are ready. Quote price/fees/refund, reserve inventory or escrow, settle once, and handle rejection, conflicts and restart. Provider-backed Store remains a different integration.
5. **Places, courts and social outings:** reconcile WORLD's actual venue/room work, existing justice/eligibility APIs and office ownership. Fill the confirmed playable gaps, including guests/capacity/seat durations, without duplicating present systems. Verify both permission refusal and a complete allowed journey.
6. **Nightlife and visuals:** add lightweight visible luxury/social effects with transparent prices and expiry. Verify names/queues/recipient visibility, lamp/placement/avatar rendering and local capture. Keep artwork and optional effects lazy; profile actual low-end devices and honor reduced motion.

Every unit ends with current compiler/build/budget checks, the relevant existing journeys, two-actor/receipt/save proof where applicable, and real mobile interaction. A sealed integrated Worker and post-adoption production continuity check remain required before a deployment claim. Source plans, partial prototypes and public author announcements do not count as delivered parity.
