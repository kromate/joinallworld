## Interpretation and remaining gaps

- No author response in the opened branches established that the requested additions shipped. The three root announcements remain claims requiring independent source/runtime verification.
- The largest themes are coherent ownership/access, reliable money and recovery, useful car ownership, visible social presence, and safe collaborative home editing. Requests for wealth scarcity conflict with requests for higher-price spectacle; they are not one consensus requirement.
- Several reports may be intended rules, stale clients, misunderstanding or actual defects. Price multipliers, investment loss, police outcomes and home retention especially require authoritative explanation before classification.
- Do not treat adult-content, gambling or crime requests as commitments or an instruction to implement them. The task was source capture only.
- Unresolved branches include low-signal tagged conversations and some one-reply discussions in the root lists. Root counters are not comparable to descendant-inclusive counts, and probable-spam controls did not reveal additional content. Image-only content was not OCR-audited. Full-tree completeness remains unproven.
