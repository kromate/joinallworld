## Housing semantics

The earlier “house purchase” wording needs correction. There are two separate systems:

- A rented-home ladder in `src/game/content/housing.ts`. `HOUSES_DATA` defines five rental tiers with weekly rent and a three-week move-in charge ([`housing.ts:20-45`](../../src/game/content/housing.ts:20)). `property.house-move` explicitly pays “landlord and agent” and changes the rented house ([`property.ts:13-18`](../../src/game/systems/property.ts:13)). The action debits `house.moveIn` and sets `state.property.house` ([`property.ts:53-65`](../../src/game/systems/property.ts:53)).
- A separate own-plot estate system in `src/game/content/world.ts`. Every life receives a free starter house on an allocated plot; upgrades buy a larger tier and incur ground rent ([`world.ts:38-51`](../../src/game/content/world.ts:38)). The own-house style has roof, wall, door, window, fence, yard, and sign choices, with prices on some changed options ([`world.ts:57-75`](../../src/game/content/world.ts:57)). `homeOwned` is only a legacy flag ([`property.ts:5-9`](../../src/game/systems/property.ts:5); [`types/life.ts:439-443`](../../src/types/life.ts:439)).

Therefore, rental move-in is not freehold purchase. Actual own-plot ownership and tier upgrades exist in source, while the user-facing Houses app’s “Homes to rent” flow is rental-only ([`HousesApp.vue:35-70`](../../src/app/features/home/HousesApp.vue:35)).
